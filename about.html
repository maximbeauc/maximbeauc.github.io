About Me

A bit about who I am, what I work with, and what I do for fun.

Hi, I'm Maxim.

I'm a second-year Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Waterloo, currently on my second co-op term. At school, I love hanging out with friends, and honestly, I love doing schoolwork. However, my favourite night of the week is Monday night at poker club.

I'm most passionate about learning new things. While embedded firmware, controls, and software development are currently my main fascinations, I'm eager to explore anything math and engineering related. Math was my favourite subject in elementary and high school, and I've always been fascinated by the logical thinking required in engineering.

My programming journey started in elementary school, where the block-coding Scratch piqued my interest. Later in high school, I built games in Java for my computer science courses and served as the Executive of the Computer Science Club. I've since expanded into C/C++, C#, Python, and web technologies, and most recently, firmware for microcontrollers.

My first co-op was as a Software Developer Student at Innovative Automation Inc., where I led cross-department Continuous Improvement projects in C#, Blazor, and SolidWorks. For my second co-op, I came back as a Controls Programming Student, getting much closer to the hardware with HMIs, PLCs, and computer vision. On top of that, I'm on the Controls Subteam at Waterloo Rocketry, writing firmware for the rocket's canards.

In my free time, I enjoy practicing Shotokan karate, where I earned a 2nd Degree Black Belt, and teaching as a volunteer instructor — which has taught me discipline, leadership, and how to work with others effectively.

I'm actively looking for my next co-op or internship opportunity, whether in southern Ontario, or anywhere else in Canada.
